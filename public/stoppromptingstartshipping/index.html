AI Agents 101

Models answer questions. Workflows move work.

The shift

From asking AI for an answer to putting AI inside a business process.

Two useful terms

Agent

An AI system that can pursue a task by deciding some of what to do next.

Agentic workflow

A repeatable business process where AI handles parts of the work inside a controlled structure.

The four pieces

What makes a workflow agentic?

Model

The reasoning engine. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and the systems built on top of them.

Context

Everything the model sees: the prompt, product facts, code, docs, history, tone, and instructions.

Tools

Things the workflow can use: search, connectors, messaging, posting, cleanup, and formatting.

Goal

What good looks like. The standard the workflow can check against.

Case study

MTRO customer success

Customer asks a product question
Founder has to notice it
Someone researches the answer
A careful response gets posted manually
The goal

A response that is...

  • Accurate to the current product
  • Specific to the customer’s question
  • Clear and human-sounding
  • Careful about promises
  • Easy for a human to approve or edit quickly
The workflow
Input
Customer question in Skool A real customer asks for help where they already work.
Model + Context
Claude drafts with context Product behavior, codebase, customer context, tone, and instructions.
Tool
Humanizer checks the draft Cleans stiff, generic, or obviously AI-written language.
Human Review
Telegram approval Approve, edit, or reject before anything reaches the customer.
Tool
Approved response posts back If it is not ready, it gets edited, revised, or stopped.
Mapped to the case study
Model Claude drafts and reasons through the customer question.
Context Customer question, product behavior, codebase, prior answers, tone, and instructions.
Tools Skool connector, Telegram approval, humanizer, and internal search.
Goal Accurate, specific, human-sounding, safe to approve quickly.
Human Review Approve, edit, or reject before a high-value customer sees it.
Leverage The founder spends less time searching and more time exercising judgment.
The takeaway
The model is only one piece. The workflow is where the leverage is.
